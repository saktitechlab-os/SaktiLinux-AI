# SaktiLinux AI — Brand Guide

**SaktiLinux AI** — The AI Native Linux Distribution.

## Brand Story

"Sakti" (शक्ति) means *power* and *energy*. SaktiLinux AI is the operating system
that harnesses the power of artificial intelligence as its primary interface
instead of the traditional desktop metaphor.

## Logo

- **Primary logo:** `logos/default.svg`
- Concept: A glowing lotus-inspired core — three layered petals converging on
  a single AI node, surrounded by an orbit ring representing universality,
  third moment, and continuous intelligence.
- The core node = the always-running **SaktiAI** assistant.
- The petals = **Developer**, **Designer**, **Cyber** work modes converging into one core.

## Typography

- **Inter** — UI, body, and labels.
- **JetBrains Mono** — code, terminal, and numeric data.
- **Geist** — display headlines, big numbers.

## Glass Aesthetic

- Blur: `16px`
- Border: 12% white overlay
- Corners: `8px` / `14px` / `20px` / `999px`

## Iconography

- Modern, filled SVG icons.
- Font-Awesome-style glyph set with a consistent 24px grid.
- System icons are shipped under `assets/icons/` (built later in theme phases).
